About Credit Fix FL

CREDITFIXFL INC, doing business as Credit Fix FL, is a credit repair company located at 816 Kazaros Circle, Ocoee, FL 34761. We serve individuals in Orlando and Central Florida who sign up with us directly.

We work only with our own clients. We do not sell, buy, or share customer information or leads, and we do not refer clients to other companies.

Our approach is straightforward: review your reports, dispute what is inaccurate, and teach you how to manage your credit going forward. No guarantees, no upfront fees.

Your Rights Under the Credit Repair Organizations Act

  • CREDITFIXFL INC does not charge or collect any fee before the services we agreed to provide have been fully performed.
  • You may cancel your contract with us without penalty or obligation within 3 business days after the date you sign it.
  • You have a right to dispute inaccurate information in your credit report by contacting the credit bureau directly. You can do this yourself, free of charge.
  • Neither you nor any credit repair company has the right to have accurate, current, and verifiable information removed from your credit report.
  • We do not guarantee any particular increase in your credit score or the removal of any item from your report.
